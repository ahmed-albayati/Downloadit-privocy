## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 8, 2026

**Downloadit** ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Downloadit app — an all-in-one video downloader for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), and YouTube.

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
Downloadit is designed to work completely offline and locally on your device after you provide a video URL. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
- Location data
- IP addresses
- Browsing history
- Downloaded videos or their content
- Any analytics or usage statistics

All video downloading, processing, and saving happens **directly on your device**. No data is sent to our servers (we do not operate any servers for this app) or to any third-party tracking services operated by us.

### 2. Permissions Used by the App
The app may request the following permissions (depending on your platform — Android/iOS):

- **Storage / Photos & Media** — To save downloaded videos to your device's gallery or downloads folder.
- **Internet / Network access** — To fetch video streams from the original platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.) when you paste a link.

We do **not** use these permissions to collect or share your personal information.

### 3. Third-Party Services and Platforms
Downloadit interacts with third-party video platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X) by fetching publicly available video streams based on the URLs you provide. We do **not**:

- Authenticate you with those platforms
- Access your accounts
- Store your credentials
- Track your activity on those services

Your use of those platforms is governed by **their own** privacy policies and terms of service (e.g., YouTube's privacy policy, TikTok's privacy policy, etc.). We are not responsible for their data practices.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Downloadit is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here in the GitHub repository with a revised "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
